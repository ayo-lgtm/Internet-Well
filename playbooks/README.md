# Playbooks

Playbooks are end-to-end professional workflows. Select one after project assessment.

## Available

- `existing-product-audit.md` — determine whether a product solves the intended problem safely and effectively.
- `launch-readiness.md` — prepare a product for release with evidence-backed gates.
- `decision-simulation.md` — conduct supervised stakeholder and scenario analysis.

## Planned

Security architecture, legal-compliance readiness, privacy, accessibility, product discovery, incident response, infrastructure selection, marketing operations, reliability, business continuity, and scaling.

## Playbook contract

Every playbook must define:

- triggering goals;
- required inputs;
- capability map;
- resource-selection rules;
- ordered procedure;
- authorization and human-review gates;
- required outputs;
- verification evidence;
- stop conditions.
