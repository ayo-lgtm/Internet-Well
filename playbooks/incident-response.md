# Playbook: Incident Response

## Purpose

Contain, investigate, communicate, recover from, and learn from a security, privacy, reliability, data, or operational incident.

## Inputs

Incident signal, affected systems, timeline, users, data, logs, owners, providers, deployment history, backups, legal obligations, and available authority.

## Workflow

1. Establish incident command, severity, scope, and a timestamped log.
2. Preserve evidence and identify what is known, suspected, and unknown.
3. Contain harm using the least destructive reversible action.
4. Investigate root cause and affected assets, users, data, and time range.
5. Coordinate security, privacy, legal, engineering, operations, communications, and providers.
6. Recover from a verified clean state and monitor recurrence.
7. Document decisions, notifications, residual risk, corrective actions, and owners.
8. Conduct a blameless post-incident review and verify prevention work.

## Outputs

Incident record, severity, timeline, evidence, containment, affected scope, recovery status, communication decisions, corrective actions, owners, and verification dates.

## Verification

Confirm containment through current telemetry; validate recovered systems; test credentials, access, data integrity, and critical journeys; verify alerts and corrective controls; retain evidence appropriately.

## Stop conditions

Stop before destroying evidence, making unsupported public claims, notifying external parties without authority, paying demands, or taking irreversible production action without incident command approval.

## Human review

Security, privacy, legal, communications, executive, and domain owners must review material incidents, regulated data, customer harm, financial loss, law-enforcement contact, and notification obligations.
