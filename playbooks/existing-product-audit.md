# Playbook: Existing Product Audit

## Goal

Determine whether an existing product solves a real user problem effectively, safely, and sustainably.

## Required inputs

Project assessment, intended users, core promise, critical journeys, business model, jurisdictions, deployment state, known incidents, and available product evidence.

## Capability map

- product value and user-journey analysis;
- architecture and dependency review;
- testing and accessibility;
- security, privacy, and legal-compliance triage;
- reliability and operations;
- AI evaluation where applicable;
- launch or remediation planning.

## Procedure

1. Restate the product's intended outcome in user language.
2. Identify the smallest set of critical journeys that must work.
3. Inspect current implementation and evidence for those journeys.
4. Separate core-value failures from optional feature defects.
5. Select registry resources only for identified capability gaps.
6. Test high-impact assumptions and failure paths.
7. Rank findings by user harm, business impact, likelihood, and remediation effort.
8. Produce a verdict: proceed, proceed with conditions, hold, or stop.
9. Provide an ordered remediation plan and verification gates.

## Required outputs

Executive verdict, core-value assessment, critical-journey evidence, capability gaps, selected resource bundle, prioritized findings, remediation sequence, launch implications, and human-review requirements.

## Stop conditions

Do not manufacture evidence. Do not equate feature count with product value. Escalate legal, financial, safety, regulated-data, or high-impact AI conclusions to qualified review.
