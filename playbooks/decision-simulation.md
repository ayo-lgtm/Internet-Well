# Playbook: Decision Simulation

## Goal

Use evidence-grounded stakeholder and scenario analysis to improve a decision without presenting simulation as prediction.

## Required inputs

Decision, options, sourced facts, assumptions, stakeholders, time horizon, success criteria, constraints, and consequences of error.

## Procedure

1. Separate verified facts, founder assumptions, and generated hypotheses.
2. Identify materially affected stakeholder roles; do not impersonate real people.
3. Define baseline, best-case, expected, adverse, and adversarial scenarios.
4. Limit agent count and authority to the minimum needed.
5. Run structured rounds: initial reaction, interaction, challenge, adaptation, and synthesis.
6. Require every material conclusion to cite a premise or be labeled speculative.
7. Run an adversarial reviewer and an uncertainty reviewer.
8. Compare options against success criteria and failure consequences.
9. Produce a confidence-rated recommendation and conditions that would change it.

## MiroFish relationship

`registry/product/mirofish.md` is an experimental reference implementation only. Do not copy its source or prompts into proprietary work. Any Internet-Well skill derived from general architectural ideas must be independently written and evaluated.

## Required outputs

Decision framing, stakeholder map, scenarios, reactions, disagreements, evidence gaps, best and worst cases, confidence, recommendation, conditions to proceed, and human-review needs.

## Stop conditions

Do not treat simulated consensus as truth. Do not use confidential or regulated data without an approved data path. Do not make legal, financial, medical, political, safety, or personnel decisions solely from simulation output.
