# Playbook: Privacy and Data Governance

## Purpose

Map personal and sensitive data, minimize unnecessary collection, and verify that product behavior, disclosures, providers, retention, and user controls align.

## Inputs

Project assessment, data inventory, users, jurisdictions, vendors, analytics, AI providers, storage, retention, deletion, legal documents, and incident history.

## Workflow

1. Trace data from collection through use, sharing, model processing, storage, export, deletion, and backup.
2. Classify purpose, necessity, sensitivity, lawful basis or permission, owner, retention, and recipients.
3. Compare actual behavior with privacy notices, consent, settings, and contracts.
4. Review access, tenant isolation, encryption, logs, subprocessors, regions, and deletion propagation.
5. Identify dark patterns, excessive collection, hidden tracking, and incompatible reuse.
6. Select only needed privacy, inventory, scanning, and governance resources.
7. Produce remediation, disclosure, vendor-review, and verification gates.

## Outputs

Data-flow map, inventory, purpose and retention matrix, disclosure mismatches, provider risks, user-rights gaps, selected resource bundle, and remediation plan.

## Verification

Test consent and withdrawal, export, correction, deletion, account closure, analytics behavior, provider calls, retention jobs, backup implications, and role-based access with non-production data.

## Stop conditions

Stop before sending real sensitive data to a new provider, deleting production records, changing legal notices, or making jurisdictional compliance claims without authority.

## Human review

Qualified privacy, legal, security, and records-management review is required for regulated data, children, biometrics, health, finance, employment, cross-border transfers, and legal compliance conclusions.
