# Playbook: Product-Market-Fit Review

## Purpose

Determine whether the product solves a meaningful problem for a reachable user segment and identify the smallest evidence-backed path to stronger value and adoption.

## Inputs

Founder thesis, users, critical journeys, analytics, interviews, support, churn, activation, retention, pricing, acquisition, alternatives, and product evidence.

## Workflow

1. Define the target user, painful situation, current workaround, promised outcome, and switching reason.
2. Separate core value from optional features and cosmetic polish.
3. Trace acquisition, activation, first value, repeated value, payment, retention, referral, and failure.
4. Analyze behavioral evidence and direct user language.
5. Identify the largest uncertainty: problem, segment, solution, trust, distribution, pricing, or execution.
6. Design the smallest ethical experiment that changes a decision.
7. Select research, analytics, experimentation, and feedback resources only for the identified uncertainty.
8. Produce a prioritized learning and product plan.

## Outputs

Core-value verdict, user segment, problem evidence, journey metrics, friction, assumptions, selected resources, experiments, decision thresholds, and product priorities.

## Verification

Use real behavior where available; reconcile analytics with user evidence; define precommitted success and failure thresholds; verify instrumentation before interpreting results.

## Stop conditions

Do not equate downloads, impressions, waitlists, compliments, or feature count with product-market fit. Stop experiments that manipulate, discriminate, or expose users to material harm.

## Human review

Domain, research, privacy, legal, and product owners must review high-impact experiments, sensitive users, regulated products, pricing claims, and conclusions that materially change the business.
