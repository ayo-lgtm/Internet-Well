# Playbook: Marketing System

## Purpose

Build an ethical, measurable marketing operating system that connects positioning, content, distribution, conversion, retention, and learning.

## Inputs

Product thesis, users, evidence, brand, channels, assets, pricing, analytics, constraints, legal claims, budget, and founder capacity.

## Workflow

1. Define segment, problem, promised outcome, proof, differentiation, objections, and prohibited claims.
2. Map awareness, consideration, activation, conversion, retention, referral, and reactivation.
3. Choose the smallest channel set based on audience access and founder capacity.
4. Create a message architecture, content system, editorial cadence, conversion paths, and measurement plan.
5. Review consent, tracking, deliverability, platform rules, accessibility, endorsements, and claims.
6. Select registry resources for analytics, email, CRM, content operations, SEO, experimentation, and privacy only where needed.
7. Run bounded experiments with precommitted metrics and stop rules.

## Outputs

Positioning, audience, message hierarchy, channel strategy, content calendar, conversion paths, selected resources, metrics, experiments, and governance.

## Verification

Validate tracking, consent, attribution limits, links, forms, deliverability, accessibility, claims, and experiment integrity; compare business outcomes rather than vanity metrics.

## Stop conditions

Stop deceptive claims, fake scarcity, purchased engagement, spam, impersonation, undisclosed endorsements, dark patterns, and any promise of guaranteed virality or revenue.

## Human review

Legal, privacy, brand, domain, and platform specialists must review regulated claims, endorsements, contests, financial or health marketing, children, personal data, and material advertising spend.
