# Playbook: Legal and Compliance Readiness

## Purpose

Identify legal and compliance obligations that may affect the product, distinguish confirmed requirements from open questions, and route issues to qualified review.

## Inputs

Project assessment, entities, jurisdictions, users, product claims, business model, data classes, providers, payments, regulated activities, contracts, policies, and launch plan.

## Workflow

1. Map entities, roles, jurisdictions, users, transactions, data, content, and regulated functions.
2. Identify potentially applicable legal domains without claiming universal compliance.
3. Compare product behavior with contracts, policies, disclosures, consent, licenses, and marketing claims.
4. Review intellectual property, open-source obligations, privacy, consumer protection, accessibility, platform terms, records, and sector-specific risks.
5. Separate mandatory blockers, counsel questions, operational controls, and optional best practices.
6. Select standards, templates, scanners, and reference resources only where evidence supports them.
7. Prepare a counsel-ready issue list with facts, sources, assumptions, and deadlines.

## Outputs

Jurisdiction and obligation map, confirmed facts, open legal questions, policy and contract gaps, license obligations, selected resources, blockers, owners, and counsel-review package.

## Verification

Verify product behavior against source documents and current official requirements; confirm effective dates and jurisdictions; test user-facing disclosures and consent; recheck third-party and open-source terms.

## Stop conditions

Stop before giving definitive legal advice outside competence, filing, signing, changing legal documents, representing compliance, or launching a regulated function without qualified review.

## Human review

Licensed counsel and relevant compliance specialists must review jurisdiction-specific obligations, regulated activities, legal documents, intellectual property, referral or fee structures, employment, finance, healthcare, and material compliance claims.
