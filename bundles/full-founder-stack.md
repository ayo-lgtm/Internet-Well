# Bundle: Full Founder Stack

## Outcome

Provide a solo founder with a coordinated, evidence-backed operating baseline across product, engineering, security, privacy, legal, accessibility, AI, operations, marketing, finance, and launch.

## Required capabilities

Project intelligence, product discovery, architecture, decision records, implementation, testing, CI, security, privacy, accessibility, legal issue spotting, AI evaluation, deployment, observability, backups, incidents, cost control, analytics, marketing, support, and continuous verification.

## Selection rules

Start with the founder's current outcome and stage. Activate only capabilities needed now or necessary to prevent material harm. Reuse adequate existing systems. Prefer the smallest compatible resources and bundles; record deferred capabilities, restrictions, and human review.

## Implementation order

1. project assessment and core value;
2. product and stack profiles;
3. architecture and critical journeys;
4. engineering and verification baseline;
5. security, privacy, accessibility, legal, and AI risk controls;
6. deployment, operations, support, recovery, and cost;
7. marketing and learning system;
8. launch review and continuous verification.

## Verification

Run the applicable playbook gates; map every critical acceptance criterion to current evidence; validate connected resources at their pins; test integrated behavior, failure, rollback, recovery, and ownership; disclose failed, blocked, and unverified work.

## Human review

Qualified specialists remain mandatory for high-risk security, privacy, legal, finance, tax, accounting, healthcare, employment, accessibility, regulated data, production infrastructure, trading, and material business commitments.
