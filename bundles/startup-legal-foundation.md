# Bundle: Startup Legal Foundation

## Outcome

Create an organized issue-and-document foundation for a software startup while clearly separating templates and operational controls from jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## Required capabilities

Entity and ownership records, intellectual-property chain, founder and contractor documentation, open-source compliance, privacy and terms alignment, vendor and customer contracts, employment classification, records retention, insurance questions, and regulatory issue spotting.

## Selection rules

Apply the product, business model, jurisdictions, and regulated-function assessment. Use verified standards and templates only as drafting aids. Never represent a template as sufficient for every company or jurisdiction.

## Implementation order

1. map entities, owners, products, jurisdictions, people, vendors, customers, data, and regulated functions;
2. inventory existing documents and missing decisions;
3. verify IP and open-source obligations;
4. align product behavior, privacy, terms, and contracts;
5. prioritize blockers and counsel questions;
6. establish ownership, renewal, and records systems.

## Verification

Confirm executed documents, parties, dates, product names, domains, actual behavior, licenses, approvals, storage, renewals, and unresolved counsel issues. Do not infer signatures or compliance.

## Human review

Licensed counsel, tax, accounting, insurance, employment, privacy, and regulatory specialists must review jurisdiction-specific documents, equity, regulated products, material contracts, fundraising, employment, and legal conclusions.
