# Bundle: Legal-Tech Launch

## Outcome

Prepare a legal-information or legal-workflow product for controlled launch without overstating legal accuracy, outcomes, attorney relationships, or jurisdictional coverage.

## Required capabilities

Jurisdiction mapping, source provenance, legal-boundary guardrails, abstention, counsel escalation, privacy, privilege-aware handling, multilingual evaluation, critical-journey testing, accessibility, security, retention, audit logs, incident response, and launch governance.

## Selection rules

Apply `profiles/legal-tech.md` plus relevant stack profiles. Use official legal sources and current effective dates. Select resources that preserve evidence and human review. Do not use simulation, model confidence, or user enthusiasm as proof of case merit or legal outcome.

## Implementation order

1. define product scope and prohibited legal functions;
2. map jurisdictions and sources;
3. test intake and critical user journeys;
4. implement abstention, escalation, privacy, and access controls;
5. evaluate languages, documents, and high-impact outputs;
6. verify accessibility, operations, incident response, and legal disclosures;
7. obtain counsel review and launch verdict.

## Verification

Test source freshness, jurisdiction mismatch, emergency and deadline paths, unsupported questions, sensitive-data flows, non-English outputs, accessibility, attorney package quality, deletion, audit logs, and provider failure.

## Human review

Licensed counsel, privacy, security, accessibility, and domain reviewers must approve legal boundaries, jurisdiction-specific content, referrals, fees, claims, and launch readiness.
