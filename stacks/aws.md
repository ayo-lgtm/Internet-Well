# AWS Stack Guide

## Detection

Look for AWS SDKs, IAM policies, CloudFormation, CDK, Terraform, Lambda, ECS, EKS, S3, RDS, DynamoDB, Cognito, CloudWatch, or deployment credentials.

## Required controls

Account and environment separation, least-privilege IAM, organization and root-account protection, secret management, encryption, logging, network boundaries, backups, recovery, cost budgets, infrastructure-as-code, provenance, and incident response.

## Compatible capabilities

Cloud architecture review, IAM analysis, infrastructure scanning, secrets detection, logging and alerting, backup and restore, disaster recovery, cost optimization, compliance mapping, and deployment verification.

## Verification

Review effective IAM; validate infrastructure plans; test logging, alerts, backups, and restore; inspect public exposure and encryption; confirm environment separation, budgets, change history, and rollback.

## Human review

Production accounts, IAM, billing, networking, regulated data, destructive infrastructure changes, and security controls require explicit authorization and qualified cloud/security review.
