# Fintech Product Profile

## Applies to

Products handling payments, accounts, investing, lending, digital assets, financial records, financial recommendations, KYC/AML, or regulated market access.

## Required capabilities

Ledger integrity, reconciliation, idempotency, authentication, authorization, fraud controls, audit logs, privacy, data residency, regulatory mapping, disclosures, transaction monitoring, incident response, backups, recovery, and model-risk controls.

## Risk model

Primary risks are loss of funds, incorrect balances, duplicate or unauthorized transactions, misleading performance claims, unsuitable recommendations, regulatory violations, market abuse, identity theft, weak partner controls, and irreversible autonomous actions.

## Completion evidence

Money movement is reconciled; failure and retry paths are tested; permissions are least-privilege; logs are tamper-evident; disclosures match behavior; partner dependencies are documented; recovery is rehearsed; and high-impact calculations are independently verified.

## Human review

Qualified legal, compliance, security, accounting, and financial professionals must review regulated activities, licensing, custody, trading, investment advice, KYC/AML, sanctions, consumer disclosures, and production money movement.
