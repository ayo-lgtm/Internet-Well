# Internal Enterprise Tool Profile

## Applies to

Tools used inside organizations for workflows, investigations, legal operations, case management, reporting, collaboration, automation, or employee productivity.

## Required capabilities

Enterprise identity, role-based access, least privilege, audit logs, retention, export, accessibility, data classification, change management, integrations, business continuity, incident response, and adoption measurement.

## Risk model

Primary risks are excessive access, confidential-data leakage, shadow workflows, inaccurate automation, conflicts with employment obligations, weak records retention, vendor lock-in, and unreviewed actions affecting customers or employees.

## Completion evidence

Roles and access are tested; sensitive data paths are documented; audit and retention work; integrations fail safely; critical workflows have owners; adoption and quality are measured; rollback and support processes exist.

## Human review

Security, privacy, legal, HR, records-management, and business owners must review systems affecting investigations, employment, privileged work, customer actions, regulated records, or production operations.
