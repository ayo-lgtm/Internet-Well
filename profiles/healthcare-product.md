# Healthcare Product Profile

## Applies to

Products handling health information, care navigation, clinical support, wellness, diagnostics, patient communications, medical records, or benefits.

## Required capabilities

Health-data classification, consent, identity, least privilege, encryption, retention, audit logs, clinical evidence boundaries, safety escalation, accessibility, reliability, incident response, and vendor/data-flow review.

## Risk model

Primary risks are harmful medical guidance, missed emergencies, inaccurate clinical claims, unauthorized disclosure, discriminatory outputs, weak consent, unsafe automation, and confusion about provider relationships.

## Completion evidence

Emergency paths are explicit; the product distinguishes education from diagnosis or treatment; health data paths and vendors are documented; access and audit controls are tested; model outputs are evaluated; and recovery procedures are rehearsed.

## Human review

Qualified clinical, privacy, security, and legal professionals must review medical claims, clinical decision support, regulated health data, emergencies, consent, and production use affecting patient care.
