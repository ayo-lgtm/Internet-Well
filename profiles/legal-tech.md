# Legal-Tech Product Profile

## Applies to

Products that organize legal information, intake users, generate legal documents, support attorneys, route matters, or help users understand procedures.

## Required capabilities

Jurisdiction detection, scope and unauthorized-practice guardrails, source provenance, abstention, privacy, privilege-aware data handling, accessibility, multilingual quality, human escalation, audit logging, document integrity, and outcome evaluation.

## Risk model

Primary risks are users mistaking information for legal advice, inaccurate jurisdictional guidance, overconfident case predictions, exposure of privileged or sensitive facts, discriminatory triage, deadline harm, attorney-client confusion, and misleading marketing.

## Completion evidence

Critical journeys are tested; legal-information boundaries are clear; high-impact conclusions abstain or escalate; sources and effective dates are recorded; privacy and retention controls are verified; non-English paths are evaluated; and qualified counsel reviews jurisdiction-specific claims.

## Human review

Licensed counsel must review legal advice boundaries, mandatory disclosures, jurisdictional content, referral and fee structures, privileged-data handling, and any feature affecting rights, deadlines, eligibility, or expected outcomes.
