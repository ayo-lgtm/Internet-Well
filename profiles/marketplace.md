# Marketplace Product Profile

## Applies to

Products connecting buyers and sellers, clients and providers, attorneys and potential clients, employers and candidates, or other multi-sided participants.

## Required capabilities

Identity and role management, listing integrity, search and ranking evaluation, payments or lead routing, moderation, fraud prevention, dispute handling, communications safety, privacy, marketplace health, accessibility, and auditability.

## Risk model

Primary risks are fraud, impersonation, discriminatory ranking, unsafe participants, fake listings, off-platform abuse, fee ambiguity, conflicts of interest, spam, data leakage, and marketplace imbalance.

## Completion evidence

Role boundaries and permissions are tested; listings and participants can be reviewed and removed; ranking behavior is measurable; disputes and reports have workflows; communications are protected; marketplace metrics distinguish supply, demand, conversion, quality, and harm.

## Human review

Qualified review is required for regulated providers, employment decisions, housing, legal referrals, financial services, healthcare, children, safety-sensitive goods, fee sharing, and jurisdiction-specific marketplace obligations.
