# Mobile App Product Profile

## Applies to

Native, cross-platform, or installable mobile products distributed directly or through app stores.

## Required capabilities

Device permissions, secure local storage, offline and sync behavior, deep links, push notifications, accessibility, crash reporting, release signing, store compliance, privacy disclosures, update strategy, and backend compatibility.

## Risk model

Primary risks are excessive permissions, insecure local data, inconsistent offline state, broken upgrades, notification abuse, platform-specific defects, store rejection, hidden tracking, and unsupported devices.

## Completion evidence

Critical journeys pass on representative devices; permission prompts are contextual; local secrets are protected; offline conflicts are tested; crash and performance evidence exists; accessibility is checked; release signing and rollback are documented.

## Human review

Privacy, child safety, payments, health, location, biometric, financial, and regulated-data features require qualified review before release.
