# Open Source Guides — running and sustaining open-source projects

## What it does
GitHub's curated guides on starting, licensing, governing, growing, and
funding open-source projects: "Starting an Open Source Project," "Legal
Side of Open Source," "Building Communities," "Getting Paid for Open
Source Work," maintainer self-care, and metrics.

## When to use
- A founder open-sourcing anything (SDKs, tools, this registry's model):
  checklist for licenses, contributor guidelines, community setup
- Deciding *whether* to open-source a component — the business-model
  discussion is sober, not evangelical `[I]`

## When not to use
- As legal advice on licensing disputes (counsel)
- Product/company strategy generally — it is scoped to open source
